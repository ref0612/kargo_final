01

Resumen ejecutivo

El consultor no nos entregó una especificación cerrada: nos entregó un esqueleto de requerimientos con preguntas abiertas, un mockup de la recolección y tablas de indicadores que aún están en borrador. Lo usamos como guía para completar el flujo que nos faltaba.

Lo que planteó

  • Logística en 3 tramos: First Mile (recolección), Mid Mile (bodega y bus) y Last Mile (entrega).
  • 4 funciones de soporte: Control Tower, Auditoría/Conciliación, Finanzas e Infraestructura.
  • Para cada actor: qué espera de la app y cómo se mide su desempeño en un dashboard.
  • Antifuga: etiquetas "1/x", ingreso exitoso y conciliación de lotes.

Lo que hicimos

  • El flujo pasó de 8 a 11 estados, con aceptación del despachador y tramo de destino.
  • Última milla con dos modalidades: con el operador o retiro del destinatario.
  • El conductor de recolección puede ajustar la cantidad de bultos y reimprimir etiquetas; la firma es obligatoria.
  • Bus con sugerencia de la parrilla y ventana de 2 horas.
  • 3 roles nuevos o ampliados: Control Tower, Auditoría y Finanzas, más el onboarding (Konnect y KAM).
  • 9 indicadores calculados a partir del registro de eventos.

01

Executive summary

The consultant didn't hand us a closed spec: he gave us a skeleton of requirements with open questions, a pickup mockup, and KPI tables that are still a draft. We used it as a guide to complete the flow we were missing.

What he proposed

  • Logistics in 3 legs: First Mile (pickup), Mid Mile (warehouse and bus) and Last Mile (delivery).
  • 4 support functions: Control Tower, Audit/Reconciliation, Finance and Infrastructure.
  • For each actor: what they expect from the app and how their performance is measured on a dashboard.
  • Leak-prevention: "1/x" labels, successful inbound scan, and batch reconciliation.

What we did

  • The flow grew from 8 to 11 states, with dispatcher acceptance and a destination leg.
  • Last mile with two modalities: delivered by the operator, or picked up by the recipient.
  • The pickup driver can adjust the package count and reprint labels; a signature stays mandatory.
  • Bus assignment with a timetable suggestion and a 2-hour window.
  • 3 new or expanded roles: Control Tower, Audit and Finance, plus onboarding (Konnect and KAM).
  • 9 KPIs computed from the event log.

Roles and onboardingDoneControl Tower, Audit, Finance, merchant registration by the KAM, and operator import from Konnect.
KPIsDoneComputed client-side; the consultant's formulas have two errors we documented.

Billing and pricingOn holdFinance is read-only and shows no prices.

05

El nuevo flujo

Este es el diagrama que el consultor dejó vacío. Antes eran 8 estados y la orden saltaba de "cargada en bus" a "entregada"; ahora son 11.

ESC = escaneo de cada bulto (usuario + fecha/hora) · FIRMA = firma de una persona autorizada, RUT verificado visualmente · 10a = última milla con el operador · 10b = retiro del destinatario · la descarga del bus no se escanea · el merchant ve solo 5 estados (Creada, Asignada, En recolección, En tránsito, Entregada).

Antes (8)Ahora (11)
creada → asignada_operador → recoleccion_asignada → recolectada → en_bodega_operador → asignada_transporte → cargada_bus → entregada creada → asignada_operador → aceptada → recoleccion_asignada → recolectada → en_bodega_operador → asignada_transporte → cargada_bus → en_bodega_destino → (ultima_milla_asignada | lista_retiro) → entregada
05

The new flow

This is the diagram the consultant left blank. It used to be 8 states, jumping straight from "loaded on bus" to "delivered"; now it's 11.

SCAN = per-package scan (user + timestamp) · SIGN = signature from an authorized person, RUT visually verified · 10a = last mile with the operator · 10b = recipient pickup · bus unloading is not scanned · the merchant only sees 5 states (Created, Assigned, In pickup, In transit, Delivered).

Before (8)Now (11)
created → assigned_to_operator → pickup_assigned → picked_up → in_operator_warehouse → transport_assigned → loaded_on_bus → delivered created → assigned_to_operator → accepted → pickup_assigned → picked_up → in_operator_warehouse → transport_assigned → loaded_on_bus → in_destination_warehouse → (last_mile_assigned | ready_for_pickup) → delivered

07

Data and rule changes

New data files

FileWhat it's for
konnect.jsonSimulated catalog of Konnect operators (fleet, timetable, crew). Operators are imported from here.
incidents.jsonIncidents: count, damage, missing, other; open or resolved, with an optional photo and a resolution note.
config.jsonBusiness rules: promised hours (48) and the bus window (2).

New order fields

  • Count: bultos_declarados (immutable), bultos_total (current), and conteo with the difference, reason, who and when.
  • Acceptance: status (pending, accepted, rejected) and a list of rejections with their reason.
  • Transport: service, departure, assignment time, load time, riesgo_sla and ventana_ok.
  • Last mile: modality, driver, vehicle, pickup point and "ready" timestamp.
  • Destination warehouse: inbound timestamp. Optional MPO. Warehouses with an optional postal code.

New rules the backend must enforce

RuleDetail
Accept before assigning a driverA driver can't be assigned until the dispatcher accepts. A rejection requires a reason and sends the order back to "created".
Count adjustmentOnly during pickup, only by the assigned driver, always with a reason. An already-scanned package can't be removed. Every change leaves a count difference and opens an incident.
Mandatory signaturePickup and final delivery aren't confirmed without an authorized person, a verified RUT and a signature. Confirmed once the active packages are scanned.
BusThe service must cover the route and the departure must exist in the timetable. Departing more than 2 h after assignment is flagged as risk. Window compliance is measured at load time.
Last-mile modalityChosen by the coordinator at assignment. With the operator: the dispatcher assigns a driver. Pickup: the warehouse worker marks "ready" and hands over with a scan and signature.
IncidentsReported by the merchant and the crews; only the Coordinator and Audit can resolve them, with a note.
OnboardingOnly the Coordinator registers merchants (valid, unique RUT) and imports operators (one-time).
Read-only rolesAudit and Finance see all orders but can't modify operations.
